Sermon Clips Uploader

Sermon Clips Uploader is a private publishing tool run by Benjamin Fiadonu. It uploads short sermon clips, with their titles, descriptions and thumbnails, to two YouTube channels that he owns and manages:

  • Men on Fire
  • Wisdom Digest

The tool uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos and set their details and thumbnails. It is used only by the channel owner, only on his own channels, and is not offered to the public.
